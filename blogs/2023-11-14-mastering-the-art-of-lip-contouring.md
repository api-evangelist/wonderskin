Lip liner is the unsung hero of the makeup world, often overlooked but essential for achieving a polished and defined pout. This versatile tool can enhance the shape of your lips, prevent lipstick from feathering, and even create the illusion of fuller lips. Whether you’re a makeup novice or a makeup artist, mastering lip liner techniques can take your lip game to the next level.
